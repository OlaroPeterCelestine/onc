The office

Support for the National Chairman, carried into every programme.

The establishment of ONC for NRM is in accordance with Chapter 4, Clause 14, Subsection 1 of the NRM party Constitution. The primary purpose of the ONC is to provide substantial support to the National Chairman in effectively implementing the party’s principles, policies, and other endeavours that contribute to the betterment of the NRM.

The National Chairman of the NRM Party is His Excellency President Yoweri Kaguta Museveni. He also chairs the National Conference and the National Executive Council. The office is led by Hajjati Hadijah Namyalo Uzeiye, Senior Presidential Advisor.
